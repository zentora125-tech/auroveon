Common questions

Everything you've wanted to ask.

No jargon. No vague promises. Just honest answers about how SEO works and what you can realistically expect.

How long before I see ranking results? +

Typically 60–90 days for technical improvements to show, 3–6 months for meaningful ranking movement on competitive keywords. SEO compounds — the results from month 3 accelerate in months 6–12. We set realistic timelines in the first strategy session based on your current domain authority and competition level.

Do you guarantee page-one rankings? +

No ethical SEO agency can guarantee specific rankings — Google controls the algorithm. What we guarantee is transparent, methodical work tied to our proven framework, and documented progress every month. Our clients average position 1–5 for target keywords within 6 months because our process is sound, not because we made promises.

What's the difference between SEO and paid ads? +

Paid ads stop the moment you stop paying. SEO builds an asset — your rankings and content equity grow over time and continue driving traffic and leads even if you pause spending. Most ambitious brands use both: paid ads for immediate revenue while SEO builds the long-term moat. We help you decide the right balance.

Do I need a new website for SEO to work? +

Not necessarily — we work with existing sites all the time. However, if your site has major technical debt (poor page speed, bad URL structure, no mobile optimisation), the SEO work pairs naturally with a web development engagement. We assess your site honestly in the audit and tell you exactly what level of intervention will deliver the best ROI.

How does AI search (SGE) change SEO? +

AI Overviews and ChatGPT citations increasingly pull from authoritative, well-structured content. The same signals that make you rank on Google — E-E-A-T, structured data, clear entity relationships, topical depth — are what make you cited in AI answers. We future-proof your content for both traditional and generative search simultaneously.
